The operational challenge

The previous signage platform made it difficult to manage content and update guest-facing information efficiently across the property. Different locations, screen formats, and event requirements added complexity to keeping menus and promotions current. The team needed a more flexible system for managing updates while maintaining clear, consistent content across the network.

My role in the transition

I helped transition the signage network to OptiSigns and adapted content for the new platform. Alongside the transition, I developed flexible layouts that supported different screen orientations, venue locations, and promotional needs.

My contribution extends beyond the initial setup. I manage the ongoing process of turning creative requests into scheduled, published, and verified screen content.
