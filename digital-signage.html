The challenge

Phoenix Convention Center, Symphony Hall, and Orpheum Theatre rely on digital displays to communicate with guests and showcase food and beverage offerings. The previous signage platform limited content management and made property-wide updates difficult. The network needed a more flexible way to support changing menus, promotions, events, and operational needs.

My approach

I helped transition the signage network to OptiSigns and developed updated content focused on clarity, consistency, and visibility. I created a flexible visual system that could accommodate different screen orientations, locations, and event requirements while maintaining each food concept’s distinct identity.

The outcome

A more coordinated workflow for a property-wide signage network.

The new workflow supports coordinated content across more than 55 displays and allows menus, promotions, and guest communications to be updated more efficiently throughout the venues. My role connects creative production with the ongoing management needed to keep those screens current.
