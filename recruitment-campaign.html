The brief

Make the opportunity
easy to understand.

The campaign introduced available hospitality roles and brought the date, time, location, and application access into a consistent message. Each format needed to work on its own while feeling part of the same recruitment effort.

My approach

  • Keep the essentials consistent. Repeat the hiring message, on-the-spot interviews, and event details across formats.
  • Design for the channel. Adapt the information hierarchy for venue screens, a printed handout, and desktop and mobile inboxes.
  • Make the next step clear. Pair application QR access with a prominent email call to action.

Design & development

A branded invitation.
Built for the inbox.

I designed and developed an HTML email that brings event details, open positions, an Apply Now button, and QR access together. The desktop and mobile layouts organize the same information for different viewing widths.

Digital signage

One campaign.
Four hospitality stories.

I designed coordinated screen graphics using hospitality and culinary imagery to highlight the range of opportunities available. Consistent placement of the event information and application QR code connects the variations while the featured photography changes.

1 / 4

Print collateral

The same message.
Ready to take away.

I adapted the campaign into an 8.5 × 11-inch flyer for printed distribution. The layout combines available roles, date, time, and location in a single reference, with a prominent QR code for applicants who want to apply before attending.

Consistent typography, photography, and Aramark’s red, black, and white palette connect the flyer to the digital materials.

Completed creative package

Connected across channels.

Campaign materials were developed for the November 2, 2026 hiring event. The completed package includes print, digital signage, and HTML email assets prepared to support recruitment outreach.

At the job fair, the Aramark hiring team screened 250 candidates and hired 85.
